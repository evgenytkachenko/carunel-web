Beadwell Words Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 5, 2026 · Last updated: July 5, 2026

Beadwell Words is a children's literacy-practice app published by Carunel LLC. Beadwell Words is designed to keep your child's practice data, profiles, and reports on this device. This privacy policy explains what data the app handles and how.

In this policy, "Carunel," "we," "our," and "us" refer to Carunel LLC.

The short version

Beadwell Words stores practice data, profiles, and settings locally on your device. There are no user accounts. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking. We do not operate servers that receive or store your child's practice data.

What data the app stores on the device

Beadwell Words stores the following data locally on the device where the app is installed. None of this data is sent to Carunel unless a parent explicitly exports and shares it.

Child profile information

  • An optional display name or nickname chosen by a parent (not required)
  • A grade band (Kindergarten through Grade 3) and session-length preference, chosen by a parent
  • A bead avatar — shape, color, eyes, and other visual style choices (an abstract illustration, not a photo)

Practice data

  • Practice sessions, individual answers, and activity history
  • Parent-facing progress reports generated from this activity history

Settings and preferences

  • App settings and preferences stored on the device
  • Parent Toolkit purchase status, cached locally so the app can recognize a completed purchase without a network connection every time

Locally generated export files

  • When a parent chooses to export data, a file is generated and stored temporarily on the device before the parent shares it using the device's sharing tools

All of this data is stored only on the device. It is not transmitted to Carunel or any third party except as described below.

What we do not do

  • We do not require or support user accounts
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services
  • We do not display advertising or use advertising SDKs
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies
  • We do not use social-media integrations or cloud sync
  • We do not operate servers that receive or store your child's practice data

Data that may leave the device

There are two ways data can leave the device, both requiring explicit parent action:

  • Parent-initiated data export: A parent may choose to export local practice data as a file using the device's built-in sharing tools. The parent chooses where to send the file. Carunel LLC does not control or monitor what happens after the file leaves the app.
  • Parent Toolkit purchase or restore: When a parent chooses to purchase or restore Parent Toolkit, the app communicates with the applicable app store (Google Play or Amazon Appstore) to process or verify the transaction. Payment-card information is handled by the applicable app store and is not received or stored by Carunel LLC.

How to delete data

Parents can manage app data from within Beadwell Words' Data & Privacy settings:

  • Remove a child profile: Deletes that profile and its associated local practice data (sessions, answers, and bead avatar). Other profiles are not affected.
  • Reset all Beadwell Words data: Removes all local profiles, sessions, answers, and settings from the device. A Parent Toolkit purchase entitlement is preserved through a reset — a valid purchase is never revoked by resetting your data.
  • Uninstalling the app: Normally removes the app's local data from the device, subject to your device's operating system and any backup behavior it performs outside our control.

Children's privacy

Beadwell Words is designed for children, with a parent or guardian involved in setup, profile creation, settings, and purchases. Children interact with practice activities; profile setup, purchases, and data management are performed by a parent or guardian.

Beadwell Words does not require an account, does not ask children for personal information, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

Beadwell Words does not integrate with any advertising, analytics, social-media, or cloud-sync services. The one exception is the applicable app store (Google Play or Amazon Appstore), which the app communicates with only when a parent purchases or restores Parent Toolkit, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or its store listing.
