Common questions

Where can I download your apps?

Our apps are available on Amazon Fire tablets, Android, and iPad. Store links will be posted on each product page as they become available.

Do your apps collect any data?

No. All of our apps are offline-first. They store data locally on your device and do not transmit anything to our servers or third parties.

I found a problem with an app. How do I report it?

Email us at hello@carunel.com with a description of what happened. Include the device and app version if you can — it helps us investigate faster.
