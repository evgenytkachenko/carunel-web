Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026 · Last updated: September 8, 2026

This page describes how Carunel LLC ("Carunel," "we," "our," or "us") handles information from visitors to this website, carunel.com. It does not cover the Beadwell, GentleClover, or QuizWell apps, each of which has its own privacy policy linked from its product page and from the footer of this site.

The short version

This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or user accounts. You can reach us by emailing us directly, or by submitting the inquiry form on the Contact page, which is processed by Formspree. We use the information you choose to send us to respond to you and manage that conversation.

How you can contact us

You can email Carunel directly at any time at business@carunel.com. This goes straight to our inbox through your own email provider — Carunel does not receive anything beyond what a normal email contains.

The Contact page inquiry form

The inquiry form on our Contact page is powered by Formspree, a third-party form-processing service. When you submit the form, the information you enter — your name, email address, organization (if provided), inquiry type, and message — is sent to Formspree, which delivers it to Carunel by email. We do not operate our own server to receive form submissions.

Formspree processes this information on our behalf and has its own privacy practices, described in the Formspree Privacy Policy.

The form includes a hidden field designed to catch automated spam submissions. It is not visible or usable by people filling out the form normally.

What we use it for

We use the information you provide — whether by direct email or through the inquiry form — to respond to your message and manage the resulting conversation, such as an organizational consulting inquiry, a partnership or media request, or app support. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties.

How long we keep it

We retain correspondence and inquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, maintain business records, and meet any applicable legal or accounting obligations. If you'd like us to delete information you've sent us, email business@carunel.com and we'll do so unless we have a legitimate reason to keep it.

What this website does not do

  • We do not use analytics or visitor-tracking services
  • We do not use advertising or advertising trackers
  • We do not use cookies
  • We do not require or support user accounts on this website
  • We do not sell or rent your information to third parties

Children's privacy

This website is a corporate and product-information site, not a service directed at children. For information about how our children's apps — Beadwell, GentleClover, and QuizWell — handle data, see each app's own privacy policy, linked from its product page.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
