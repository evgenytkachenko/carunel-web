Calm math practice for curious kids.

A tablet-based math practice app that feels focused, gentle, and clear. Short sessions, thoughtful progression, and a quiet parent area for guidance and insight — all offline, all on your device.

Offline-first · No data collection

Practice that follows readiness

Beadwell provides structured math practice for children in Kindergarten through Grade 3. Activities follow Montessori developmental principles: hands-on counting and matching for younger children, then a gradual transition to symbolic math as readiness grows.

Sessions are short — 5 to 15 questions — with gentle feedback and no time pressure. Children advance when they're ready, not when they've filled a progress bar.

Designed differently

Beadwell is built on a simple idea: children learn well when they feel calm.

No game economy

No coins, levels, streaks, or leaderboards. Progress is measured by understanding, not points.

No child-facing scores

Children see gentle encouragement and natural feedback — never accuracy percentages or rankings.

Mastery before advancement

New concepts appear when skills are solid, not when a set number of questions have been answered.

Personal, not generic

Parent-created missions with personal messages and family photos — not app-generated prizes.

What's inside

Calm practice sessions

Short, focused sessions with no timers, no pressure, and gentle feedback. Activities feel like working with materials, not playing a game.

Thoughtful progression

15 activity types across four developmental tiers. Concrete before abstract, mastery before advancement — following Montessori principles from counting objects to equation balance.

Parent tools and insight

Per-skill accuracy, trends, and session summaries in plain language. Actionable suggestions you can turn into custom practice missions in one tap.

Offline-first privacy

All data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics, no telemetry. This is a product commitment, not a temporary limitation.

Guidance for parents, calm for children

Beadwell keeps the child experience focused and pressure-free, while giving parents a protected space for guidance, setup, and insight.

Learning landscape

See where your child stands across every math area — which skills are comfortable, which are developing, and what comes next. No scores, just clear developmental stages.

Session detail

After each session, see what your child practiced, how comfortable they seemed, and which skills are becoming natural — in honest, gentle language.

Actionable suggestions

When the app notices an area that could use more practice, it suggests a focused mission you can create in one tap — with the right skill and level already chosen.

The result is a different kind of learning app: less pressure for children, more clarity for parents.

Built for trust

Beadwell has no internet permission in its production build. There are no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting, no authentication systems, and no API endpoints. Your child's practice data, your family's celebration photos, and your settings all stay on your device.

You can export your data at any time through the parent settings. You can reset everything with a single action. And if you uninstall the app, every trace of it leaves with it.
