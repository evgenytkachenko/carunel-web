Leadership & Practice

Hyper-Agile Quality Engineering™

Developed by Evgeny Tkachenko, this operating model connects product intent, risk, validation, automation, release readiness, and production learning. It reflects a leadership approach built around evidence and shared ownership.

  • Connect product intent with clear test expectations.
  • Focus validation on risk and change impact.
  • Strengthen automation and continuous quality signals.
  • Improve release-readiness decisions.
  • Learn systematically from production feedback.
  • Build shared ownership across product, engineering, and quality.

Evgeny Tkachenko — Quality Engineering Leader & Author

Evgeny Tkachenko is a software engineering and quality leader with more than twenty years of experience, author, speaker, and originator of the Hyper-Agile Quality Engineering™ framework and Hyper-Agile Quality Loop. He is the author of three books on Quality Engineering, AI-powered applications, and AI-accelerated software delivery, including the Apress book Hyper-Agile Testing.

Learning Apps

Calm learning products for children and families.

Evgeny’s hands-on product work includes Beadwell, GentleClover, and QuizWell: learning apps available across iOS, Android, and Amazon Appstore. Each combines thoughtful design, privacy-conscious choices, and respect for children’s attention.

Beadwell Calm, Montessori-aligned math practice for ages 5–9. GentleClover Calm, offline activities for ages 3 and up. QuizWell Calm, parent-guided quiz practice for ages 5–10.
