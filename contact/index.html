Common Questions

Where can I download your apps?

Beadwell, GentleClover, and QuizWell are available on the App Store, Google Play, and the Amazon Appstore. Store links are on each product page.

Do your apps collect any data?

Our apps store data locally on your device by default and do not require accounts. See each app's Privacy Policy for the specifics of what stays on-device and what, if anything, is needed to process a purchase.

I found a problem with an app. How do I report it?

Email us at business@carunel.com with a description of what happened. Include the device and app version if you can — it helps us investigate faster.

Do you offer individual coaching or consulting?

No. Hyper-Agile Quality Engineering™ consulting is designed for organizations and cross-functional teams. Individual coaching and consumer consulting are not currently offered.
