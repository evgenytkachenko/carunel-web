A calm place to play

GentleClover is a quiet activity app designed for children ages 3 and up. Over 20 activities across four areas — matching, puzzles, drawing, postcards, travel scenes, and more — all in a gentle, unhurried space.

There are no timers, no scores, no flashing rewards. Children explore at their own pace, and every activity ends with a warm sense of completion.

Four areas to explore

Each area offers a different kind of quiet engagement, with age-appropriate content for younger and older children.

Play

Matching, sorting, memory, odd one out, and more — gentle games that build focus.

Puzzles

Jigsaw scenes, dot-to-dot shapes, and spatial challenges at every age level.

Create

Draw and doodle, build scenes, place stickers, and make postcards to save.

Travel

Travel pairs, destination puzzles, seek-and-find scenes, and packing adventures.

Designed for calm

No pressure, no noise

No timers, no scores, no leaderboards, no flashing rewards. Activities end with a gentle sense of completion, not a push to play again.

Parent-friendly setup

Parents choose the age band, manage sound settings, and view activity summaries — all behind a simple parent gate. Children only see activities.

Age-appropriate content

Age bands starting from age 3 adapt activity complexity so younger children aren't overwhelmed and older children aren't bored.

Offline-first privacy

All data stays on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. This is a product commitment, not a temporary limitation.

Everything included

GentleClover is a paid app with everything included. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no ads, and no locked content. You pay once, and your child has access to every activity and every piece of content from day one.

No monetization messaging is ever shown to the child.

Built for trust

GentleClover works entirely offline. There are no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting, no authentication systems, and no API endpoints. Your child's play data, saved creations, and settings all stay on your device.

You can reset all app data with a single action in the parent area. And if you uninstall the app, every trace of it leaves with it.
